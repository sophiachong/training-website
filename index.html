Bridge Week

  • Dates: 21 September to 27 September 2026
  • Main goal: absorb the 5K test before starting the 10K build.
  • Running: keep runs easy and short enough that you feel better by the end of the week.
  • Strength: keep one or two light maintenance sessions, with lower-body work optional.
  • Next step: start the 10K block on Monday 28 September.

Strength During the 5K Block

  • Keep two sessions per week: enough to maintain strength, not enough to sabotage quality running.
  • Reduce lower-body cost late: Weeks 9 and 10 intentionally pull back the lower-body work.
  • Main idea: one squat-based day and one hinge-based day, both with a press and a pull.
  • If running quality drops: reduce gym volume before adding more recovery debt.
