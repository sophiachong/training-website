🌙 Nightly Mobility Routine

For desk workers & runners • 12-15 minutes before bed

Why this routine? Sitting all day tightens your hip flexors, rounds your shoulders, and shortens your hamstrings. Running adds stress to your calves, hips, and IT band. This routine targets all of it.

Hold each stretch for 60-90 seconds. Breathe slow and deep. This isn't about pushing hard — it's about unwinding.

⏱️ 15-20 min
🎯 13 stretches
🛏️ Before bed
🔧 Band optional
🎵 Play Chill Yoga Music on YouTube Music →
1

90/90 Hip Stretch

60-90 sec each side
📹 1 min demo

Sit with both legs bent at 90°. Front shin parallel to your body, back shin perpendicular. Lean gently over front leg, then rotate toward back leg.

→ Opens hip internal & external rotation (tight from sitting + running)

2

Half-Kneeling Hip Flexor Stretch

60-90 sec each side
📹 Watch Demo

Back knee down, squeeze glute on that side, shift hips forward slightly. Raise same-side arm overhead and lean away for deeper stretch.

→ Undoes desk-chair hip flexor tightness

3

Pigeon Pose

60-90 sec each side
📹 Yoga With Adriene

Classic pigeon: front leg bent in front of you, back leg extended. Fold forward over front leg. Alternative: Figure-4 on your back if pigeon is too intense.

→ Glutes and deep hip rotators

4

Supine Hamstring Stretch

60-90 sec each side
📹 30 sec demo

Lie on back, loop band or strap around foot, extend leg toward ceiling. Keep opposite leg flat on ground. Gently pull to increase stretch.

→ Hamstrings without straining lower back (uses your band!)

5

Supine Spinal Twist

60-90 sec each side
📹 Yoga With Adriene

Lie on back, drop both knees to one side, arms out wide, look opposite direction. Let gravity do the work — don't force it.

→ Thoracic spine and lower back — decompresses after sitting

— Neck & Shoulders —
6

Chin Tucks

10-15 reps, slow
📹 30 sec demo

Lie on back or sit tall. Gently draw chin straight back (making a "double chin"). Hold 3-5 seconds. Don't tilt head up or down — just glide it back.

→ Fixes forward head posture from screens, strengthens deep neck flexors

7

Neck Side Stretch + Levator Scapulae

45-60 sec each side
📹 2 min demo

Drop ear toward shoulder, gently press with same-side hand. Then angle nose toward armpit and press again for levator scapulae. Breathe and relax into it.

→ Releases neck tension and that tight muscle connecting neck to shoulder blade

8

Thread the Needle

45-60 sec each side
📹 30 sec Short

On all fours, slide one arm under your body, lowering that shoulder to the floor. Let your upper back rotate. Reach the other arm overhead for a deeper stretch.

→ Thoracic rotation + shoulder opener — counters hunched desk posture

9

Doorway Pec Stretch

45-60 sec each side
📹 1 min demo

Stand in doorway, forearm on frame at 90°. Step through gently until you feel stretch across chest. Try arm higher and lower to hit different pec fibres.

→ Opens chest, pulls shoulders back — essential for desk workers

— Transition to all fours —
10

Cat-Cow

8-10 slow breaths
📹 1 min demo

On all fours, inhale and arch spine (cow), exhale and round spine (cat). Move slowly with your breath. Let your head follow naturally.

→ Spine mobility, calming for nervous system

11

Child's Pose with Lat Stretch

60-90 sec total
📹 30 sec Short

Sink hips back to heels, arms extended forward. Walk hands to each side (30 sec each) to stretch lats.

→ Lats, shoulders, lower back

12

Wall Calf Stretch

45 sec each position, each leg
📹 1 min demo

Toe on wall, heel on floor, lean in. Do with straight leg (gastrocnemius), then with bent knee (soleus). Both matter for runners!

→ Calves and achilles — crucial for runners

— Final relaxation —
13

Legs Up the Wall

2-3 minutes
📹 1 min demo

Lie with legs resting up the wall (or on your bed frame). Arms relaxed at sides. Just breathe. This is your reward for showing up.

→ Relaxes nervous system, drains legs after running, promotes sleep

💡 Tips for Success

  • Dim the lights — helps signal to your body it's wind-down time
  • No forcing — this isn't stretching for gains, it's recovery
  • Breathe deep — slow exhales activate your parasympathetic system
  • Skip what hurts — sharp pain means stop; dull stretch is fine
  • Consistency > intensity — 10 min daily beats 30 min once a week
  • Pair with sleep routine — do it at the same time each night
