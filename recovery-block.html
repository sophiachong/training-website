🌿 Recovery + maintenance month — built for post-HM reset, busy work weeks, and staying sane

Recovery & Maintenance Block

4 Weeks • Recover from the half • Stay moving • Start the strength block in mid-May feeling human

Goals: absorb the HM, stay consistent through work peak, keep some strength touch, arrive fresh for the proper strength block

Week 1

Pure recovery week. Let the race land, keep movement gentle, and do not audition for another life crisis.
RECOVER
Mon
Rest: Rest or easy walk
Tue
Rest: Rest or 20–30 min gentle walk / spin
Wed
Easy: 20–30 min very easy jog if legs feel okay
Thu
Rest: Rest or easy cross-training
Fri
Easy: 25–35 min easy run
Sat
Strength: Optional mobility + light activation only
Sun
Long: 35–45 min easy run
Aim: feel better by the end of the week, not fitter.

Week 2

Start feeling like a runner again. Easy consistency, light strength touch, no big drama.
RESET

Rest: Rest or walk

Strength: Second light / moderate strength session

Aim: return to routine without creating fresh fatigue.

Week 3

Maintenance week for a busy life. Keep the engine warm, but let work win the argument when needed.
MAINTAIN

Rest: Rest

Sun
Long: 60–70 min easy long run
If work gets ugly: cut the quality first, then shorten the long run.

Week 4

Hold steady, stay sane, and finish the month ready to begin the proper strength block in mid-May.

Wed
Strength: Light strength session

Fri
Rest: Rest or mobility

Strength: Optional second light strength session
Sun
Long: 60–75 min easy long run
Aim: finish this block wanting to train again, not needing another holiday.

Block rules

  • Main priority: recover from the half and survive work peak without letting training become another stressor.
  • Run intensity is optional: one light quality touch most weeks is plenty.
  • Strength is maintenance only: keep some load, but leave plenty in reserve.
  • Protect in this order: sleep, easy consistency, long run, light strength, optional quality.
  • If the knee gets grumpy: reduce intensity first, then trim volume, and keep the single-leg / calf basics.

This month is not about proving fitness. It is about emerging in mid-May feeling steady, healthy, and ready to actually start the strength block properly.
